## 📌 Project Overview

**ProductScope** is a SaaS product analytics project focused on understanding user behavior, product engagement, and subscription performance.

The project uses **Python, Pandas, SQL, and Power BI** to explore how users interact with a digital product, identify patterns in feature usage, evaluate conversion funnels, and analyze customer retention and revenue performance.

By combining data analysis, SQL-based business queries, and interactive dashboards, ProductScope transforms raw product data into meaningful business insights that can support better product and business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze user acquisition and signup trends.
- Evaluate user activation and conversion funnel performance.
- Measure adoption of individual product features.
- Understand user engagement and product usage patterns.
- Analyze trial-to-paid subscription conversion.
- Evaluate customer retention and churn behavior.
- Explore subscription revenue and Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).
- Identify opportunities to improve product engagement and business growth.

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The project uses four structured datasets representing different aspects of SaaS product activity.

| Dataset | Description |
|---|---|
| `users.csv` | User information and signup details |
| `events.csv` | Product interaction and feature usage events |
| `sessions.csv` | User session activity and engagement |
| `subscriptions.csv` | Subscription and payment-related information |

These datasets are analyzed together to understand the customer journey, from initial signup and product interaction to subscription conversion and churn.

## 🔍 Key Business Questions

ProductScope explores business questions such as:

1. How are new users acquired over time?
2. What percentage of users become activated?
3. Where do users drop off in the conversion funnel?
4. Which product features receive the highest adoption?
5. How does user engagement vary across different user groups?
6. What percentage of trial users convert into paid subscribers?
7. How does customer activity change over time?
8. What is the customer churn rate?
9. How does churn impact subscription revenue?
10. What is the Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)?
11. Which areas of the product offer the greatest opportunities for improvement?

## 📊 Key Metrics & Analytical Areas

| Metric | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Users | Measures the overall user base |
| Activation Rate | Measures the proportion of users completing defined activation criteria |
| Feature Adoption Rate | Evaluates adoption of individual product features |
| Conversion Rate | Measures movement between funnel stages |
| Trial-to-Paid Rate | Evaluates conversion from trial to paid subscriptions |
| Engagement | Measures product usage and user activity |
| Retention Rate | Evaluates how effectively users continue using the product |
| Churn Rate | Measures the proportion of customers discontinuing subscriptions |
| MRR | Measures recurring subscription revenue |

*Metric definitions and calculation logic are based on the analytical methodology used in the project.*

## 💡 Business Value

ProductScope demonstrates how product analytics can help SaaS businesses make informed decisions.

- **Improve User Activation:** Identify opportunities to reduce friction in the onboarding journey.
- **Optimize Conversion:** Understand funnel drop-offs and improve trial-to-paid conversion.
- **Increase Feature Adoption:** Identify underutilized features and opportunities to improve product discovery.
- **Strengthen Engagement:** Understand product usage patterns and customer activity.
- **Improve Retention:** Explore churn behavior and identify potential retention opportunities.
- **Grow Subscription Revenue:** Monitor recurring revenue and evaluate subscription performance.
